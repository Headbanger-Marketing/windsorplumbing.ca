FAQs

Questions Windsor Homeowners Ask

Answers to the questions Windsor homeowners ask us most.

Are you licensed and insured to work in Windsor?

Yes. We are licensed plumbers working across Windsor and Essex County, fully insured, and every job requiring a permit is filed with the municipality and inspected under the Ontario Building Code.

Do you handle emergency plumbing calls in Windsor?

Yes. Burst pipes, no water, sewer backup, and failed tanks are priority calls, and because our crews work the city daily we can usually reach any Windsor address quickly.

My water heater is leaking right now. How fast can you replace it?

Often the same day. Common tank sizes ride on our trucks, so we can drain the old unit, swap in the new one, and restore hot water in a single visit most of the time.

Is my older Windsor home a candidate for repiping?

If it still has original galvanized supply pipe, yes, especially with rusty water, dropping pressure, or repeat leaks. We can repipe the whole house or just the worst branches in PEX or copper.

What does my Windsor basement need to stay dry?

A working sump pump on a battery backup plus a permitted backwater valve covers the two ways basements flood here, groundwater and sewer surge. We assess both and quote the gaps honestly.

Is Windsor water hard enough to need a softener?

It is moderately hard, enough to scale fixtures and heater elements over time. A right-sized softener plus carbon filtration for late-summer taste makes a noticeable difference in city homes.

How do you price plumbing work?

We quote before we start, explain what the quote covers, and stand behind it. Whether the job is a toilet swap or a full repipe, there are no surprise charges on the invoice.

Which areas do you serve around Windsor?

All of Windsor plus the surrounding Essex County communities, including LaSalle, Tecumseh, Amherstburg, Lakeshore, Essex, and Leamington.
