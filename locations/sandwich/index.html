FAQs

Sandwich Plumbing Questions

Do you replace original galvanized pipe in Sandwich homes?

Yes, it is some of our most common work here. We repipe Sandwich houses in PEX or copper under permit, restoring pressure and stopping pinhole leaks.

Can you camera an old Sandwich sewer line?

Yes. We run the camera the full length, show you roots, sags, or breaks, and quote the repair that matches what the line actually needs.

Do Sandwich basements need sump protection?

The low riverside grade makes it wise. We install sized sump pumps, battery backups, and backwater valves so storm water stays out of the basement.

Do you serve Sandwich for emergencies?

Yes. Sandwich sits minutes from our daily route, and burst pipes, backups, and no-water calls are handled on a priority basis.
